Optional Visual Enhancer preset for FFmpeg Forge 3.0.1

FFmpeg Forge writes its own Visual Enhancer preset for the Deblurring section (FocusPulsar).
To use your own settings instead, export a preset from the Visual Enhancer app and save it here as:
  focuspulsar.json    (Deblurring - FocusPulsar)
OptiPulsar needs no preset (built-in engine, or VE_CLI.exe flags).
